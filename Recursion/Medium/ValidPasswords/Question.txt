A password contains n positions . For every positions you can place ABC .
But the password follows these rules:-
(i) Two B's cannot be ajacent.
(ii)'B'can not come just after 'A' .
(iii) CCC can't be the password

To find:
No. of valid passwords of size n.

Example:
if n=3 , ans = 16
if n=2 , ans = 7
