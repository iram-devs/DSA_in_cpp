Given a Family of Engineers and Doctors.

Conditions :
(i) First child of an Engineer must be Engineer and second child will be Doctor.
(ii) FIrst child of Doctor must be Doctor and second child will be Engineer.
(iii) Family always starts with Engineer.

This scenario can be represented in the form of tree:

                         E
                        / \
                       E   D
                     / \   / \
                    E  D  D   E
                   / \ /\ / \ / \
                  E  D D E D E E D

Given : an integer 'level' and an integer 'position'.
To Find: The Profession at given level & position . Return in the form of string such as "Engineer" or "Doctor".
